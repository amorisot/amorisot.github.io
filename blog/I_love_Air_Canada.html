    I love Air Canada

    Published: Jun 11, 2026

    Every so often, there is a story in the news about some absurd air travel mishap. Surprisingly frequently, it is about Air Canada.

    To illustrate: - Air Canada customers kicked off plane for refusing vomit-covered seat - The largest gold heist in Canadian history was an inside job at an Air Canada warehouse - An Air Canada pilot mistook the planet Venus for an oncoming plane and dove, injuring 16 - An Air Canada captain allegedly flew over 900 flights across 17 years without a valid pilot's licence - Air Canada denied boarding to a $4.5M cello with its own confirmed seat, cancelling a sold-out recital - Air Canada's app secretly recorded users' screens, exposing passport and credit card numbers - Air Canada baggage handlers caught on video tossing luggage off a six-metre staircase - Air Canada abandoned a 14-year-old at Toronto airport after cancelling her flight - An Air Canada plane taxied for takeoff with a ground worker trapped inside the cargo hold, freed only after passengers heard him banging - An Air Canada passenger fell asleep and woke up alone on a dark, locked, empty plane parked for the night - Air Canada told a tribunal its own chatbot was "a separate legal entity responsible for its own actions" — and lost - Air Canada let a greyhound out of its crate at SFO; he bolted and was never recovered alive
