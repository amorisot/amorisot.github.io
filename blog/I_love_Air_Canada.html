    I love Air Canada

    Published: Jun 11, 2026

    Every so often, there is a story in the news about some absurd air travel mishap. Surprisingly frequently, it is about Air Canada.

    To illustrate:

    • Air Canada customers kicked off plane for refusing vomit-covered seat
    • An Air Canada pilot mistook the planet Venus for an oncoming plane and dove, injuring 16
    • An Air Canada captain allegedly flew over 900 flights across 17 years without a valid pilot's licence
    • Air Canada denied boarding to a $4.5M cello with its own confirmed seat, cancelling a sold-out recital
    • Air Canada baggage handlers caught on video tossing luggage off a six-metre staircase
    • Air Canada abandoned a 14-year-old at Toronto airport after cancelling her flight
    • An Air Canada plane taxied for takeoff with a ground worker trapped inside the cargo hold, freed only after passengers heard him banging
    • Air Canada told a tribunal its own chatbot was "a separate legal entity responsible for its own actions"
    • An Air Canada passenger fell asleep and woke up alone on a dark, locked, empty plane parked for the night
    • Passenger forced to drag himself off Air Canada flight after airline failed to provide wheelchair assistance
